# Open Problem 4: A New Problem, You Are in the Wrong Place

Everyday Workplace Communication · Module 1 · Segment 5: Reading Signs, The Wrong Room · **Stage 5.7** (COM-M1-OP-04), in the Swift AI Academy frame.

The whole activity is one plain HTML page (`index.html`), with its pictures and sounds inside the file. No framework or build step is needed. `Module1_Open_Problem_4_Stage5.7_You_Are_in_the_Wrong_Place.html` is the same file, kept for downloading. It opens with a double-click.
The page is generated from `module1-segment1/` with `node scripts/plain-page.mjs scripts/op57-plain.template.html <out.html>`. Do not edit it by hand.

## Publish on GitHub + Vercel

1. In Vercel choose **Add New → Project** and import this repository.
2. Set **Framework Preset** to **Other**. Leave the build command and output directory empty.
3. Choose **Deploy**.

GitHub Pages also works: Settings → Pages → deploy from the `main` branch, root folder.

## How it works

1. **Your first move.** The opening story uses the three painted scenes from the client's asset package, with soft cross-fades:
   - Ananya inside the doorway of Room 2, mid-morning, after registration;
   - the empty room, with stacked chairs and a blank board;
   - Ananya by the open door, with the empty corridor beyond and her empty speech bubble.

   The narrator reads each caption as it types in, and Ananya says "Oh. This is the wrong room." As the package asks, the right room is never named. The story plays once, on its own where the browser allows sound. Browsers block sound until the visitor taps or presses a key, so otherwise the story runs silently and the first tap starts it again with sound. The sound button sits in the caption bar, so it never covers the picture. People who prefer reduced motion see the last scene.

   Then the script's question: "You realise you are in the wrong room right now, and no one is around yet. What do you do first?" The learner writes their first move (5 to 50 words). A reason is welcome but not required. An optional "Need help getting started?" panel gives the package's three hints, which do not suggest a strategy.
2. **Compare and reflect.** The learner sees their answer and a short feedback message. The message is built from an internal check: a sensible first move (several are accepted, including waiting nearby or checking the admission folder) and a finished sentence ("I would ask" must say who). A reason is praised when given. **The key words and the checks are never shown to learners**; staff can find them in the separate checking-metric document. Then come the brief's three cards, with the package's explanations: go back the way you came, re-read the signs, and look for someone to ask. They are labelled as possibilities, not corrections. Optional voice practice is available, and the learner can choose "Try another first move".
3. **What comes next.** The package's question: "What would help you decide what to do next?" (3 to 30 words). It is checked internally for one helpful thing: the signs or room numbers, the learner's papers, someone to ask, a call or message, or a place they know. A short phrase such as "A sign in the corridor." is accepted here. Then come ideas to compare.

At the end: "Practice complete", **Download my notes** (a text file) and **Delete my saved work**.

- **Only real answers are accepted.** Before any feedback, the page checks that the answer is a real answer to the question. It must be mostly real English words; its content words (not I, would, the…) must mostly be real everyday words or words from the question; it must not repeat the same word or be just "I do not know"; it must be written as a sentence; and it must be on the question (the main idea, such as a first move, not only a reason such as "because…", or one of the question's topic words). Otherwise the learner stays on the question with a short message ("This does not answer the question yet…"), and the attempt is saved in the record as not valid. For example, "apple orange mango yes and" and "I would apple orange because grapes banana okay bye" are not accepted. Weak but genuine answers, such as "I would try the next door", are still accepted and get gentle feedback.
- **Not graded:** a "Practice · not graded" badge and no score. Attempts are saved in the browser as `5.7.open-problem-log`. Nothing is sent anywhere; recordings are never saved or uploaded.
- The top bar shows "About 10 minutes" and never counts down. There is no Pause button and no time limit, as in the other Module 1 pages.
- No contractions are used anywhere on screen.
